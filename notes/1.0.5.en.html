What's new in Canif 1.0.5

  • “Trackpad” sounds: 13 very short sounds (Tick, Plop, Bubble, Ding, Knock-knock, Zip, Cricket…) played by the haptic motor of the Force Touch trackpad. You hear them even with the Mac muted, and feel them under your finger. They are offered wherever you pick a sound. Without a Force Touch trackpad, a similar Mac sound plays instead.
