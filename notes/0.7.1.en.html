What's new in Canif 0.7.1

  • Notch: it stays anchored to the Mac’s notch. A small extension signals activity at rest; hovering widens it moderately. The large panel no longer opens automatically: click to access the widgets.
  • Network devices: your custom LanScan names help identify the type, brand and model when explicitly named, even offline. Multi-word rooms are preserved and the evidence is explained in the device details. These names stay on your Mac and are not sent to Fingerbank.
