What's new in Canif 1.5.0

  • New tools: Calendar (the date in the menu bar, the month and your events in one click) and Rename (dozens of files at once: replace, number, case, date, with preview and undo).
  • Wi-Fi: built-in speed test (download, upload, latency, responsiveness), with history.
  • Keep awake: sleep timer, the sound fades out, the music stops and the Mac goes to sleep.
  • Focus: dim everything except the active window, during a session or all the time.
  • Clipboard: collections to keep what you paste often.
