What's new in Canif 1.0.3

  • New command palette: ⌃⌥⌘Space opens a Spotlight-like search field to launch any tool or action (Keep awake 1 h, a favourite DNS, a capture…), tolerant of typos and accents; the shortcut can be changed in General. Canif is also lighter when idle: fewer wake-ups with the screens off, slower system readings in Low Power Mode, slow commands moved off the main thread, log size capped.
