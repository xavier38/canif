What's new in Canif 0.7.0

  • Discover network devices, show their MAC addresses and find your names imported from LanScan. Group them by room or person and monitor arrivals and departures. Replaces LanScan.
  • Battery: low and critical alerts with adjustable thresholds and sounds, a large orange or red banner without animation, a notification and a flashing knife. Alerts clear when you connect the charger.
  • Notch: the panel appears below the menu bar to keep its contents accessible.
