What's new in Canif 0.7.31

  • Notifications: a dedicated section now appears below Home. Configure each notification family’s color, notch dot effect, dot count and pace, visibility in the notch panel and Canif list, and critical alerts.
  • Notifications in the notch panel and Canif list have an X to acknowledge them. Calendar reminders keep their X visible in the notch panel even when side dots are disabled.
  • Home is less crowded: general settings now have their own section. Notification groups cover calendar, charger, battery, Bluetooth, focus sessions, microphone and camera, network, web pages, and system events.
  • Charger notifications wait for a stable reading to avoid transient changes. Updates are checked at launch and every 12 hours.
