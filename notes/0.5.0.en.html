What's new in Canif 0.5.0

  • Automatic updates: Canif checks for a new version every day and installs it in one click.
  • Each tool you turn on in the menu bar has its own icon again, placed right next to Canif's.
  • Right-click Canif's icon: a list of all your tools.
  • Every tool has a direct shortcut to its settings, including the chevron bar and the clipboard history.
  • A more compact menu that fits the screen height; the tool list in the window scrolls.
  • Web apps: delete an app from the menu (trash on hover or right-click).
  • Notch: the calendar shows up as soon as access is granted.
  • Battery: the power adapter no longer switches off and on in a loop once the limit is reached.
