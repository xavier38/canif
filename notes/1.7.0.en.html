What's new in Canif 1.7.0

  • Wi‑Fi: settings by network. When you join a known network, Canif applies its settings (DNS, volume, mute, Keep awake).
  • A tool can stay on without appearing in Canif's menu (“In the menu” checkbox).
  • Musical keyboard light uses less energy.
  • Canif is now Mac only: the Linux and Windows versions are discontinued.
