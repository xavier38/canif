What's new in Canif 0.7.57

  • New Convert tool: hold ⌥ while dragging files in the Finder (or press ⌃⌥Space on the selection, or right-click > Services > Convert with Canif) and a ring of conversions opens under the pointer. Images (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, ICNS and ICO icons, PDF, smaller size, email, no EXIF), PDFs (pages to images, merge, compress), videos (MP4, 720p, HEVC, animated GIF, sound only), sounds (M4A, WAV, AIFF, iPhone ringtone), documents (PDF, Word, ODT, RTF, HTML, text), reading the text in an image, ZIP archives. Everything happens on the Mac and originals stay untouched. FFmpeg, if installed, adds MP3, WebM, FLAC and OGG.
