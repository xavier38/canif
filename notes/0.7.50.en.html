What's new in Canif 0.7.50

  • Nothing is lost anymore when Canif restarts: “Keep Awake” (including unlimited), the chosen sound output and input with their volumes, Screen Color’s manual, one-off or paused protection, alerts already seen, notch notifications, and several display choices (Wi-Fi, Uninstall, DNS, capture tool and colour).
  • Clipboard: the history can no longer be erased by a size change before it has loaded, and it is fully saved when Canif quits.
  • macOS Low Power Mode in the Battery menu and in the notch (Never, Always, On battery, On power).
