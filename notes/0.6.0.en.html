What's new in Canif 0.6.0

  • New Stickies tool: sticky notes on your desktop (6 colors, rich text, images, always on top), saved automatically, with ⌃⌥N and import from the macOS Stickies app.
  • A wider Canif menu with bigger icons.
  • Tools sorted alphabetically within each category.
  • Anonymous statistics are now on by default (turn them off in Home › Privacy).
