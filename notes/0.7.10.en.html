What's new in Canif 0.7.10

  • Notch: new notes open in front, ready to type, then return to their normal level when you switch windows.
  • The Battery card lets you set the charge limit from 50 to 100%, using the same settings as the Battery tool.
  • Volume and brightness: readable levels with percentages, previews without changing levels, and monitoring separate from optional macOS indicator replacement. Failed adjustments fall back to macOS, with clear Accessibility permission status.
