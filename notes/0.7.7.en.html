What's new in Canif 0.7.7

  • The dropdown menu now orders icons exactly like the app: by category, then alphabetically by the full name in the selected language. Shortened menu labels no longer change that order. Custom ordering remains shared with the window.
  • Microphone and camera: changing settings after disabling the tool no longer brings the badge back. Late callbacks are ignored after stopping. Separate switches monitor the microphone and camera; disabling monitoring removes its badge and blocks its notifications, sounds and new log entries.
