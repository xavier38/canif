What's new in Canif 0.7.68

  • Canif uses much less when idle: the menu bar only redraws the icon that changes, the notch no longer rewrites its notifications on every display and animates its dots through the system, music and DNS are no longer re-read for nothing, the System tool slows down when not shown, and the knife appears before tools start. Calendar notifications: alarm button always visible, with a clearly visible card in the notch (5 min to 1 h, or before it starts) that keeps the notch open while you choose; the notification comes back at the chosen time, even after a restart.
