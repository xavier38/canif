What's new in Canif 0.7.12

  • Notch: Sound, Keep Awake, Dictation, Microphone/Camera, Clocks, WiFi, System and Web Alerts cards connected to the tool settings. New cards are offered in that order; existing custom order and hidden cards are preserved.
  • Quick actions: area or window capture, colour picker, left half, right half and maximise. The notch collapses and returns keyboard focus before the action. Disabled tools can be enabled explicitly. Hovering starts no microphone, camera or network scan.
  • Sound: audio chains resume after zero volume or mute without having to reselect the speakers. A stopped or failed capture no longer leaves the original sound muted; explicit per-app muting is preserved.
