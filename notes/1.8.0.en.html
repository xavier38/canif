What's new in Canif 1.8.0

  • New Presentation mode tool: one click hides desktop icons and the Dock, turns on Do Not Disturb, keeps the screen on and pauses notifications. It can turn on by itself with a projector.
  • Anti-theft: the Mac locks when your iPhone or Apple Watch moves away, and can arm the alarm at the same time.
  • Clearer home page: each tool lists what it contains.
