What's new in Canif 1.0.0

  • Canif reaches 1.0 after a large audit (code, memory, CPU, security). It uses far less when idle, calendar notifications have a real snooze (alarm card in the notch, 5 min to 1 h, ringing even when the app is quit), each notification is dismissable with a cross in the menu, dictation no longer steals focus, telemetry is now off by default, the battery helper checks who talks to it, an unreadable data file is never overwritten, and only one instance of Canif runs at a time.
