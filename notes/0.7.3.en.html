What's new in Canif 0.7.3

  • Devices: added sorting by name, with ascending or descending order for every criterion.
  • Local import of Home Assistant names, areas and models matched by MAC address. Personal names keep priority, no commands are sent to home automation, and importing does not change presence observations.
  • Notch: its hover width adapts to the information, keeping the title and artist or calendar event readable. Click to open the lower panel.
  • Comparisons and names of replaced apps have been removed from the home screen. Name import labels are neutral.
