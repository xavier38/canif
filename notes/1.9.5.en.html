What's new in Canif 1.9.5

  • Modding, "Tap your Mac": removed on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 to M4). These Macs have no reliable shock accelerometer, so vibrations were mistaken for taps and could even lock the screen on their own. It stays available on older MacBooks with a hard drive.
  • Icons: 15 modules get a new hand-drawn icon (Wi-Fi, Clocks, DNS, Brightness, External displays, Sound, Dock preview, Dictation, System, Notch, Web apps, Screenshot, Bubble level, Tidy windows, Battery), and the icon tiles are now opaque so every icon stays clearly visible whatever the module's color. The calendar keeps its date tile.
