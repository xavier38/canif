What's new in Canif 1.9.3

  • Battery: important fix for charging during sleep. Charging was no longer resumed while asleep as soon as a limit was set, even well below the threshold — so a Mac plugged in with the lid closed would not recharge. Charging is now only paused during sleep once the threshold is actually reached. (After updating, the Battery pane offers "Update the helper": one click, admin password, and it's fixed.)
