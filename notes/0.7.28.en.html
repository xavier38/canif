What's new in Canif 0.7.28

  • Notch: the opening delay defaults to 300 ms and remains adjustable from 0 to 1 second in 100 ms steps.
  • Reduced repeated media updates and unnecessary lookups during scrolling for window gestures.
  • Accessibility: clearer selection announcements, higher-contrast permission text, reordering without dragging and an explicit close button for the notch panel.
