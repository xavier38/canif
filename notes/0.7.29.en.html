What's new in Canif 0.7.29

  • Accessibility: text adjustable up to 200%, layouts adapted to smaller screens, stronger contrast, keyboard controls and respect for Reduce Motion.
  • Ordering: handles on the left of tools, notch modules, cities and DNS favourites; saved positions and an alternative to dragging.
  • Charger: connection and disconnection messages also appear while the notch is expanded or pinned, including the active charge limit when below 100%.
  • Dictation and web alerts: operations completing after a tool is disabled no longer cause delayed pastes, notifications or cache reloads.
