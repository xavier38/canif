What's new in Canif 1.9.7

  • Icon consistency: "Keep awake" (the cup) and the Calendar now sit on the same neutral tile as every other module, with adaptive strokes and text (light in dark mode, dark in light mode). No more coffee-colored cup or clashing calendar.
  • Notifications in the dropdown: a module with a notification is now surrounded by a red frame that blinks around its icon while the notification is active — you spot it right away.
