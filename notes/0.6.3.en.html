What's new in Canif 0.6.3

  • Alerts: when something needs your attention, the knife blinks, the tool concerned gets a red badge and one click opens it: new version, lost permission, battery too hot or charge limit not applied, disk almost full, app stuck at 100% CPU, WiFi lost or very weak, DNS replaced, “Keep awake” ended, app trashed with its files. Each alert can be turned off in Settings.
  • The Canif menu closes as soon as you click elsewhere.
  • Uninstall warns before starting when Full Disk Access is needed.
