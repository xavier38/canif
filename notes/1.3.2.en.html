What's new in Canif 1.3.2

  • New tools: Anti-theft (the Mac screams if someone moves it, unplugs it or closes the lid; only unlocking stops it) and Spirit level (to a tenth of a degree).
  • New Mods tool: the screen creaks like an old door when you open it, the keyboard pulses with music, and the ⇪ light plays your messages in Morse.
  • Signals on the Mac: every tool that alerts you can vibrate the trackpad, blink the ⇪ key light or flash the keyboard, with the pattern of your choice.
  • Optional spoken announcements for web alerts, low battery and calendar, at a volume set relative to the Mac’s.
  • Battery: choose the MagSafe LED (off, green, orange, or green once the charge limit is reached). The helper moves to version 7: Canif offers to update it.
