What's new in Canif 0.7.20

  • Notch: connecting the charger also shows the active charge limit beside the battery percentage; it is hidden at 100%, when disabled or during a one-time full charge.
  • The two dots blink red for unread Canif notifications. Their subjects appear at the top of the hover panel; clicking opens the relevant section. Hovering does not mark them as read.
  • Canif menu: tools keep their place when disabled. A switch lets you stop or re-enable them directly; their controls are not loaded while disabled.
