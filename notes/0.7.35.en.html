What's new in Canif 0.7.35

  • Idle notch dots return when a notification’s side indicator expires, even while the notification remains unacknowledged.
  • Dots on each side of the notch are stacked vertically. Choose 1, 2 or 3 dots per side.
  • The name of a connected or disconnected Bluetooth device now also appears first in the drop-down list.
