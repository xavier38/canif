What's new in Canif 0.7.33

  • Notch — configure idle dots even while hidden: choose their color, count and effect. The empty preview no longer shows a misleading colored dot.
  • Each notification group now has a notch-dot duration: from 1 second to 1 hour, or until acknowledged. When the time expires, the dots stop while the notification remains in Canif until acknowledged.
  • The selected duration appears immediately in the notification preview. Alerts, the notch panel and the list keep their independent destination settings.
  • Choose among five dot sizes: ×1, ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5 and ×3, for each notification group and for idle dots.
  • Bluetooth notifications in the notch now put the device name first, followed by its connected or disconnected status.
