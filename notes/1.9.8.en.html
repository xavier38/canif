What's new in Canif 1.9.8

  • Icons: 24 modules get a new icon from your choices, and every module is now drawn (no more emoji). Network devices becomes a green radar on a black tile; Sound, Web alerts, Anti-theft, Rename and Sticky notes switch to shaded, gradient versions; Wi-Fi (arcs), Cut & paste (scissors), Caps, Focus (target), Uninstall (box), Color picker (rainbow drop), Convert, Keyboard & mouse, Abbreviations, Clipboard, Modding (knobs), Notch, External displays, Presentation mode and Tidy windows get their new drawing. The calendar stays as is.
