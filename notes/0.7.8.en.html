What's new in Canif 0.7.8

  • Microphone and camera: Enable alerts now also controls the central badge. Editing or submitting text no longer forces a preview; only Try starts an explicit preview.
  • Notch: one panel on hover and click, with one Transfers card for files and copy progress. Notes use Canif’s shared store, with create, open and previous-note recovery without duplicates.
  • Mirror: explicit activation, a Turn camera off button and shutdown when closing. Try width shows a white outline for five seconds, even with the tool disabled. Activity previews use a visible temporary card.
