What's new in Canif 0.7.32

  • Notifications: see effect changes immediately in a live preview, with clearer descriptions. Pulse fades out for one second and back in for one second, making a two-second cycle.
  • Idle dots: choose whether to show them, their color, count and effect, separately from alerts. Bluetooth and new network device alerts now show the available device name.
  • Display: choose 80% or 90% text size directly in General.
  • Stability and checks: improved notch settings refresh and verified layouts at the requested sizes.
